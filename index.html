HVAC in Ohio: A No-Heat Checklist Before You Call

Some no-heat faults have a safe homeowner check. Others mean shut it off at the breaker and stop. Work down this list, and stop at the first item that tells you to.

1. Is the thermostat actually calling for heat?

Set it to Heat and a few degrees above room temperature, and check that no schedule or hold is overriding it. A blank screen usually means dead batteries; swap them first.

2. Still blank after new batteries?

The thermostat has probably lost its 24-volt supply, often from a blown low-voltage fuse on the furnace control board. If a replacement fuse blows again, stop: that is a short in the thermostat wiring, not a part to keep swapping.

Many pre-1990 Ohio homes have no common wire at the thermostat, which is why a new smart thermostat can fail to power up even when the furnace is fine.

3. Is the furnace door seated?

The door has a safety switch that cuts power when the panel is loose. Make sure it is pushed fully home.

4. Has the condensate float switch tripped?

On a high-efficiency furnace, a clogged trap or a drain line frozen in an unheated basement can trip the float switch and shut the furnace down. Look for a kinked or iced line and any code flashing on the board. Never thaw anything with an open flame.

5. Is the filter loaded and are the registers open?

A packed filter or closed supply and return registers make the furnace overheat and shut off on the limit switch. Swap the filter and open the vents.

6. Are the outside vent pipes clear?

After a snowfall or a freeze-thaw swing, clear snow and ice from the sidewall intake and exhaust terminals by hand, from outside.

7. If you heat with propane or oil

Check the tank gauge. A low tank or regulator trouble in deep cold, or a clogged oil filter or nozzle, is a different job from a gas furnace fault, so mention your fuel when you call.

8. Did the breaker trip?

Reset it once. If it trips again, leave it off. If you smell gas, see scorched wiring or smell burning at any point, stop the checklist and get help.
